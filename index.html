What’s a Level Up?

A level up is a hard thing we work toward. We set a tough goal, map out a path to get there, and adapt when the failures show up. We don’t say “failure is not an option.” We say “failure is the only option,” because that failure feeling is a signal that shows us exactly where a weak spot may be hiding.

Quests can come in the mail or in person. Ellie’s handstand quest came with a worksheet that mapped out an early path, and finishing it earned her $5. The real prize comes when she holds a 10-second handstand: a salad bar party at Jason’s Deli (per her request) and a surprise gift! So far she’s been at this for about three months. Ellie’s hard work is the definition of the word perseverance.

Joejoe and Windzay travel a lot, so this helps keep us connected. Our favorite thing in life is watching everyone level up. We build each quest around the person’s own interests. Ellie loves to cheer, so hers is a handstand. Maverick loves engineering, so his is binary code. The big ones end with a party, and every failure along the way counts as progress.
