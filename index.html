What’s a Level Up?

A level up is a hard thing we work toward. We set a tough goal, map out a path to get there, and adapt when the failures show up. We don’t say “failure is not an option.” We say “failure is the only option,” because that’s how you learn.

Quests come in the mail or in person. Ellie’s handstand quest (we call her June Bug) came with a worksheet, and finishing it earned her $5. The real prize comes when she holds a 10-second handstand: a salad bar party at Jason’s Deli and a surprise gift!

Joejoe and Windzay travel a lot, so this helps keep us connected. Our favorite thing in life is watching everyone level up. We build each quest around the person’s own interests. Ellie loves to cheer, so hers is a handstand. Maverick loves engineering, so his is binary code. The big ones end with a party, and every failure along the way counts as progress.

The Broken Off-Switch

Adam Shuaib has sat in about 5,000 meetings with people starting companies. He noticed the ones who did amazing work had almost always obsessed over something that seemed pointless, like memorizing every NBA player at 11, building their school out of Lego, collecting thousands of train tickets, or spending years perfecting a fantasy football formula.

He says that shows the hardest skill there is: sticking with something others might call boring for longer than it seems to deserve. He calls it a “broken off-switch.”

I thought of you two.

June Bug, you’ve practiced handstands for months. Kick up, fall down, kick up again. Then one day you held one for four whole seconds. Now you’re going for ten.

Maverick, you’re learning binary code, a whole language of 1s and 0s, and you can build anything out of Legos. Binary code may look like nothing at first, but every computer and video game runs on it. Maybe one day you can build me a computer out of Legos!

Here’s the secret: the so-called boring part IS the level up. When practice feels dull, you’re not doing it wrong. You’re right in the middle of it.

Keep that off-switch broken!

We’ll be cheering the whole way.

Love, Joejoe and Windzay
