What’s a Level Up?

A level up is a hard thing we work toward. We set a tough goal, map out a path to get there, and adapt when the failures show up. We don’t say “failure is not an option.” We say “failure is the only option,” because that’s how you learn.

Quests come in the mail or in person. Ellie’s handstand quest (we call her June Bug) came with a worksheet, and finishing it earned her $5. The real prize comes when she holds a 10-second handstand: a salad bar party at Jason’s Deli and a surprise gift!

Joejoe and Windzay travel a lot, so this helps keep us connected. Our favorite thing in life is watching everyone level up. We build each quest around the person’s own interests. Ellie loves to cheer, so hers is a handstand. Maverick loves engineering, so his is binary code. The big ones end with a party, and every failure along the way counts as progress.
