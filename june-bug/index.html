Ellie

Ellie is a brainiac, an athlete, and a cheerleader on an all-star cheer team. She has worked on her handstand for about three months so far. She learns by doing, and she seems fearless. She’s a competitive risk taker who isn’t afraid to ask for what she wants out of life, and to work hard for it. That includes competing with her big brother, Maverick!

We love all of these things about this amazing athlete. Props to her parents for fostering these incredible traits!
