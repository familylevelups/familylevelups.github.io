June Bug

June Bug is a cheerleader on an all-star cheer team. She worked on her handstand for three months. She learns by doing, and she seems fearless. She already seems to be calculating some risks. She is a risk taker and competitive, and she is not afraid to ask for what she wants.

She is an extrovert who fights for attention when her big brother is around.

She has a spelling-bee-champion vocabulary, the vocab of a 30-year-old. At 7 she used the word “context” in a sentence. She also knows every word to Smashing Pumpkins’ “1979.”
